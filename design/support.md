# Scoranger

**Ask for the arrangement you want, in words, and get it engraved.**

Scoranger is a music score arrangement tool for iPad. Bring in a score, tell it
what you want in plain English, and it rewrites the notation for you. "Keep the
violins, turn the viola and cello into an accordion left hand, add chord
symbols" is a real request it can carry out.

The arranging is done by real music software, not by a language model writing
notes. The chat understands what you asked for and then calls the tools that do
the work, so the notation that comes back is correct notation rather than a
guess at it. Every change makes a new version, and you can always go back.

## What Scoranger can take in

| Format | Notes |
|---|---|
| MusicXML (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`) | The best kind of file to bring. Everything works. |
| MIDI (`.mid`) | Comes in as notation. Expect to tidy the rhythms. |
| ABC (`.abc`) | Tunes from sites like thesession.org. |
| PDF | Either as a book to read and mark up, or converted into editable notation. |
| Photos (JPEG, PNG, HEIC) | A picture of a page, which can also be converted. |

Open a file from Files, drop it on the app, or use the share sheet from another
app.

## The things people ask about first

**Do I need an account?**
No. Scoranger works fully without one, forever. Your library is on your iPad.
Sign in only if you want to share a set list with other people.

**Does it work offline?**
Almost entirely. Importing, arranging, engraving, playback, pencil marks and
export all run on the iPad with no network. Two things need one: the chat, and
converting a scan into editable notation.

**I imported a PDF and I cannot edit the notes.**
A PDF is a picture of music until it is read. Open it and tap **Make editable**.
The page goes to our server, comes back as notation, and then behaves like any
other score.

**The conversion got things wrong.**
It will, sometimes. Automatic reading of printed music is good on clean
engraved pages and much weaker on handwriting, faint photocopies, dense piano
scores and anything photographed at an angle. Check the result against the
original before you rely on it. A flat-on, well-lit photograph of a clean page
converts far better than a hurried one.

**The chat is not doing what I asked.**
Be concrete. Name parts by the names Scoranger shows in the score's info
screen, and say which bars. "Move bars 21 to 36 of the viola down an octave"
works better than "the viola is too high in the middle". If it still refuses,
it usually says why.

**Can I change which model the chat uses?**
Yes, in **Settings → Model**. The default is Google's Gemini Flash. The others
are from Anthropic, Moonshot AI, Alibaba and DeepSeek. They behave differently;
if one is being unhelpful on a particular job, try another.

**Does my music get sent anywhere when I use the chat?**
A description of it does: the title, composer, part names, instruments, ranges,
bar counts, key and meter, plus what you typed. The notation itself is not
sent. Full detail is in the privacy policy, and it is worth reading if this
matters to you.

**I undid something and want it back.**
You probably have not lost it. Every arrangement keeps its whole version
history, and you can open any earlier version from the version list. Nothing is
edited in place.

**Playback sounds wrong / silent.**
Check the mixer: parts can be muted individually. Check the iPad's silent
switch and volume. If a part plays with the wrong instrument, the instrument is
set per part and can be changed.

**Sharing a set list.**
Sign in, open the set list, and share it. That gives you a link to send. Anyone
who has the link and is signed in to Scoranger can join, up to twelve people
including you, and the link stops working after seven days. If you would rather
be specific, you can invite one email address instead, and only that address
can use the invitation. Everyone in the set list can add arrangements, reorder
them, and make their own pencil marks, and everyone can see whose marks are
whose.

**Someone sent me an invitation and tapping it does nothing.**
Install Scoranger first, then tap the link again. If you signed in with Apple
using Hide My Email, tell the person inviting you the relay address Scoranger
shows in **Settings → Account**: that is the address the invitation has to be
sent to.

**Books and reference editions do not share.**
That is deliberate, not a bug. Books you have imported and other people's
editions you keep for reference stay on your iPad and have no share button
anywhere.

**How do I delete my account?**
**Settings → Account → Delete my account.** It happens immediately, in the app.
Your library on the iPad is not touched. Set lists you own that other people
are in are handed to the next person who joined rather than destroyed.

**How do I get my music out?**
Export to PDF or MusicXML, per part or for the whole score. MusicXML opens in
MuseScore, Sibelius, Dorico and Finale.
