Stance

Musicians write in the margin. This option gives the app one: a 360pt column on the right, a shade darker than the page, where every sort, filter, ☰, share, version list and settings section opens. The page beside it keeps its width and its scroll position; the margin has one width whatever it holds, and its contents are set as text, not as controls: 14.5pt rows, a clay dot beside the current one, no boxes. There are no borders anywhere in the app. Surfaces meet as tones: ground, panel, well. Radii are a quiet 6pt. Type is the personality: Space Grotesk carries row names and the margin's headings, and the numeral is an oversized 28pt folio in the row's gutter, the way a page number sits outside the text block.

Disclosure pattern, in one sentence: every action opens in the margin, a fixed 360pt column at the right, so the page beside it keeps its width and its place; closing the margin gives the width back.

Transport and mixer are two ledger lines under the page: the transport is a 52pt line always present in the score view; the mixer is a second, 110pt line that appears under it, knobs in a row. Both end where the margin begins.

Performance mode folds the ledger lines and the margin away. A clay bookmark peeks in at the top right; tap it to come back.

Components sheet

How the rules produce the screens above. Palette and type roles are the current tokens; the margin and the folio are new.

Surfaces as tones

groundpanelwellpaperclaystrongtint

The page is ground, the margin is well, anything lifted or selected is panel. No lines: line and line2 are retired from the UI and kept for the thumbnail's page edge. band is unused.

Shape tokens

rCtl
6pt
rRow
8pt, on the selected row only
edges
none
shadow
none; the app has no floating surface left
margin
360pt, 24pt inner padding, opens and closes in 220ms with the page re-centring in step
control
40pt; 36pt in the ledger
row
64pt min, no divider

Grid and centring

grid
12 columns, 24pt gutters; the page takes 8 columns when the margin is open and all 12 when it is closed; 32pt side margins on the page, 24 in the margin
centring
Text is centred by its x-height, not its box: every control sets line-height equal to its own height and lifts the label 1pt; glyphs are 18pt squares centred on that same line. A row's text block is centred on the thumbnail's vertical middle, and the folio's baseline sits on the name's baseline.
rhythm
every vertical measure is a multiple of 8

Buttons: text first

ImportSort nameOpenDelete

A button at rest is a word with a glyph, no box. Active takes panel; primary is the only filled button on a screen. Hit areas stay 44pt whatever is drawn.

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: a Margin column on every screen that today has a reveal, a push for a short list, or a docked panel: sort and filter, the ☰ reveals, the title switcher, Versions, Parts, Set lists, Settings sections, the invitation and OMR offers. The thumbnail strip becomes a vertical grid inside it. Settings becomes a list-and-margin pair. The mixer's window shell (drag, park, clamp, collapse) goes; the strip is re-laid horizontally as a second ledger line.
  • Tokens only: radii to 6 and 8, every 1pt border to clear, PanelToggle to a capsule, the segmented control to underlined tabs, row names to Space Grotesk 600 15, the numeral to a 28pt folio in a 56pt gutter (which retires rowMenuInset arithmetic for the leading edge).
  • Unchanged: the score canvas and gestures, push navigation for objects, the knob, the system pickers, the identifiers.
  • Risk: the margin costs the page 360pt whenever it is open; at compact width (iPhone) the margin becomes a full-width anchored panel by the IPHONE_0.6.14 §6.1 rule, which is a second layout to build and test. The two-people meta line and share glyph are exactly FIREBASE §6A.2.
