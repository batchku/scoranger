Stance

Turn the card over. The page you are on never changes size and nothing on it moves out of the way, because the thing you tap becomes its own options: a row turns over and its 56pt footprint fills with the three or four buttons it had behind the ☰; the Sort button turns over and becomes the four sorts, side by side, in the same 44pt bar; the Versions row turns over into a strip of version chips. Turn it back with the ✕ that always sits where the original control was. Where the options are more than a row can hold (fourteen versions, nine settings sections) the turned-over row scrolls sideways inside itself. The forms follow the motion: every control is a capsule, every card has 14pt corners, the numeral is a clay badge you could flip. It is the most compact of the five, and the most playful.

Disclosure pattern, in one sentence: what you tap turns over in its own footprint to show its options, scrolling sideways if it must, and turns back; nothing else on the page changes size or position.

Transport and mixer are two faces of one floating deck, a 64pt capsule at the bottom centre: the transport face at rest, the mixer face when you turn it. It is the one thing in the app that floats, and it floats because it belongs to no page.

Performance mode shrinks the deck to a 44pt coin in the bottom right corner showing the play state. Tap it and the deck unfolds.

Grid and centring

grid
8pt; 20pt side margins; reading column 760; 8pt between capsules, 24 between clusters
centring
Everything inside a capsule is centred on the capsule's geometric centre. Labels are set at line-height 1 so the glyph box, not the line box, is what is centred. A glyph-and-label pair shares one baseline and gets 14pt before the glyph and 16pt after the label, so the visual weight sits central. A capsule's height is always even so its radius resolves to whole points.

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: a Turnover modifier for rows and bar buttons (two faces, same frame, a 180ms cross-fade, ✕ in the trigger's slot) replaces every inline reveal and the in-place member ☰; the version and setlist pickers become chip strips; Settings becomes a capsule strip over one card. The deck is a new floating capsule that absorbs the transport bar, the mixer window and the thumbnail strip; the mixer's drag, park and clamp go.
  • Tokens only: rCtl 2 to 999, rPanel 3 to 14, control borders to clear, PanelToggle to a capsule, row name weight 500 to 600, the numeral to the badge view in bars and heads, BandHeader to the sentence-case section header.
  • Unchanged: the score canvas and gestures, push navigation for objects and for All N, the knob's geometry and drag, the system pickers, the identifiers.
  • Risks: a turned row is dense at AX text sizes; by IPHONE_0.6.14 §6.3 rule 4 it must fall back to a vertical list at AX1 and above, which is a second layout for the same face. The floating deck over a continuous score can cover the last system, so the canvas reserves 78pt at the bottom as it did for the pill.
