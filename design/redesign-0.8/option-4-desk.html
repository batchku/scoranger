Stance

The cosiest of the five. The ground is the palette's band, a shade deeper than today's, and every screen is a sheet of panel lying on it with 20pt corners and a soft shadow, the one exception to the flat rule and a deliberate one: paper lifts off a desk. Going deeper never stacks or pushes: the next sheet slides in from the right and lies beside the one you were on, which stays visible as a 48pt spine at the left edge with its name running up it. Tap the spine to go back, or swipe. Two sheets fit on an iPad at once (a list and its detail, a detail and its versions), so the awkward third way is simply the second sheet. The numeral is stamped on the paper in a clay frame, the way a library stamps an accession number.

Disclosure pattern, in one sentence: anything deeper slides in as a new sheet beside the one you are on, which stays visible as a spine at the left edge; sheets never stack, dim or push the page down.

Transport and mixer live in the desk's drawer, a 60pt strip with rounded shoulders across the bottom of the score. Pull it and the mixer comes out under the transport; pull again for the page thumbnails.

Performance mode clears the desk. A dim clay lamp switch at the bottom left, 40pt, brings the drawer and the bar back.

Components sheet

How the rules produce the screens above. The palette is the current one with the desk on band; the sheet, the spine, the drawer and the stamp are new.

Surfaces, re-cast

band · deskpanel · sheetwell · controlpaper · pageclaystrongtint

Same hex values, different jobs: band is the desk, panel the sheets, well the controls on them, paper still only the score. ground is used for the desk on iPhone, where a sheet fills the width and needs less contrast.

Grid and centring

grid
the desk has 20pt margins and 20pt between sheets; a sheet has 20pt inner margins; 8pt grid inside
widths
a choice sheet is 320 or 340; a detail sheet 400; the leading sheet takes the rest; at most two full sheets plus a spine
centring
A label is centred by its cap height inside its control; icon buttons centre the glyph's bounding box in a square; any pair of controls side by side shares one height (44) and one baseline. A sheet header's title baseline aligns with the baseline of the buttons on the same line.

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: a Desk container replaces the per-screen NavigationStack presentation: the stack still holds the route, but the desk lays its top two routes side by side and folds the one before them into a spine, with a horizontal pan between. Every inline reveal and docked panel becomes a sheet on the desk. The drawer absorbs the transport bar, the mixer window (drag, park and clamp go) and the thumbnail strip.
  • Tokens only: the app background from ground to band, rCtl 2 to 12, rPanel 3 to 20, control borders to clear, the two shadows, PanelToggle to a capsule, BandHeader to the sentence-case header, the numeral to the stamp view in bars and heads, sheet titles to 22pt.
  • Unchanged: the score canvas and gestures, the knob, the system pickers, the identifiers, the route model.
  • Risks: the side-by-side desk is the most layout work of the five and it is a different layout again on iPhone, where a sheet fills the width and the spine becomes a back button. Shadows on panel over band must be checked for banding on older iPads. The desk's deeper ground reduces the contrast of ink3 text on it to 3.3:1, so ink3 stays supplementary, as the spec already requires.
