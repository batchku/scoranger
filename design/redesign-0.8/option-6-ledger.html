Stance

Typographic, ruled, quiet. The library is a ledger: full-width lines at 68pt, ruled with hairlines, the name set in Space Grotesk 600 at 17pt so it reads from a stand, and the numeral as a 30pt folio in a 56pt gutter. There are no boxed controls in the body; buttons are words with glyphs and take a well fill only when they mean something. What made round 1's Margin work is kept and tightened: the next level opens in a 380pt panel at the right, and the block that opens is tied to its row by a clay leader line and a dot, at the row's own height, so the eye and the pencil travel sideways a few centimetres and never down. A row's own actions (Open, Arrangement, Remove, up, down) appear inside the row when its ☰ is tapped and take the space the meta text had. Nothing appears at the bottom except the one drawer.

The drawer is one 56pt line under a 2pt clay rule: set list step, transport, one knob per part with the LED in the centre of the dial, a tempo knob, bar position, and the panel toggles. Nothing below it ever grows.

Performance mode leaves a 28pt clay ring at the top right, where the panel's corner was. Tap it to come back.

Grid and centring

grid
24pt side margins; a 56pt gutter for the folio; 8pt inside controls
centring
Text centres on its x-height: line-height equals the control's height and the label lifts 1pt. The folio's baseline sits on the name's baseline. A knob's LED is the dial's exact centre and the label centres under the dial.
travel
A row's actions appear where its meta was, so the finger moves along the row, never off it; a panel block opens at the row's height.

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: a right Panel with row-anchored blocks replaces every reveal, the version and setlist pickers, the thumbnail strip, the OMR offer and the settings dock; Row gains an actions face; the drawer merges the transport bar and the mixer window into one 56pt line (the mixer's drag, park and clamp go; the knob keeps its 14pt-per-unit drag and gains a tap-to-mute on the LED).
  • Tokens only: radii 2 and 3 to 10 and 16, borders to clear, toggle to a capsule, the numeral to the 30pt folio in a 56pt gutter, row height to 68, row name to Space Grotesk 600 17, BandHeader to the ruled section header.
  • Risk: a knob at 32pt with a 44pt group is at the floor of MIXER_WINDOW §13.2; at AX sizes the drawer's knob group must become a scrolling list, as §13 already requires for the strip.
