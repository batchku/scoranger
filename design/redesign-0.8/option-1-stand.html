Stance

A music stand has a shelf, and the shelf is where your hands go. The score is the page on the stand; the transport, the mixer, the page thumbnails, every menu and every manager live on one shelf along the bottom edge. The shelf is as tall as what it holds, up to 300pt, and it scrolls inside itself, so a menu of three rows is a shelf of three rows and never half a screen. The page above it re-centres once when the shelf changes height and otherwise never moves. Forms are soft: 10pt controls, a 16pt shelf, no hairline borders on controls at all, tone against tone. The numeral is the clip that holds the page to the stand.

Disclosure pattern, in one sentence: anything that opens, opens in the shelf at the bottom, sized to its content and capped at 300pt, scrolling inside itself, so the page above never moves.

Performance mode drops the shelf to a 5pt clay lip at the bottom centre; a tap or a pull brings it back. That lip is the only chrome left.

Grid and centring

grid
8pt; 20pt side margins; reading column 720 centred; the shelf's body shares the 20pt margin
centring
A label is centred on its cap height, not its line box: line-height 1, then padding split so the cap sits 1pt above geometric centre (Inter's descenders pull it down). Glyphs are 18pt squares centred on the same axis. Icon-and-label pairs get 12pt before the glyph and 14pt after the label.
heights
every control and row height is a multiple of 4

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: a new Shelf container replaces the inline reveal bands (sort, filter, add, title switcher, member ☰) and the docked OverlayPanel settings; the transport and mixer move into it, so MixerWindow's drag, park and clamp go and the strips stay. Settings becomes an index-and-pane screen. The thumbnail strip becomes a shelf tab.
  • Tokens only: rCtl 2 to 10, rPanel 3 to 16, control borders to clear, PanelToggle to a capsule, BandHeader to the sentence-case section header (same call sites), the numeral to the clip view where it sits in a bar or head.
  • Unchanged: rows, the score canvas and its gestures, the knob strip, push navigation, the system pickers, every identifier the tests address.
  • Risk: the shelf takes the bottom 64pt of the score view permanently, which the ink bar also docks to; the ink bar rises above the shelf as it rose above the pill.
