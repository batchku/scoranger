Corrections since option 8

C1 · The panel is paper-coloured, not whiteThe right panel is panel like the page beside it. White is for the score page only, as the brief requires.
C2 · No tab columnThe vertical tabs on the panel's spine are gone. The panel shows what the tapped row opened, headed by its name, and closes with Done or with the row's ✕.
C3 · Knobs have no pointer tickThe clay arc alone carries the level; the LED is the centre and the mute.
C4 · A selected row is a flat bandTint the width of the page, no ring, no rounded corners, no indent. The row's contents do not move when it is selected.
C5 · Performance mode has a labelled button"Perform" in the score bar's right group, a capsule with the glyph and the word.
C6 · The score bar leaves with a back arrow‹ and the name of where you came from: the set list, the piece, or Library. Never ✕.
C7 · The search field never wrapsIts label is one line and it is never narrower than 240pt. When the panel is open, the tool row gives search the first line and the buttons the second.
C8 · Nothing is cut offWhen the panel opens, the page lays itself out again at the narrower width: the tool row wraps, long meta text ends in an ellipsis, the ☰ stays inside the page. No control is ever half-hidden under the panel's edge.
C9 · Filters come from the data modelType (MusicXML, PDF, MIDI, photo), composer, instrument, tag, and status. Each is a capsule with a count, grouped; instruments come from the parts snapshot of each arrangement's latest version.
C10 · Date addedEvery library row carries two dates in mono: when it last changed, with its version, and when it was added (its first version). Sort gains Date added.
C11 · How selection startsEdit in the tool row, or a long press on any row, which enters Edit with that row already checked. Done or a tap on the empty page leaves it.
C12 · The book has a filmstripEvery page of a book as a 20pt thumbnail in a full-width strip under the pages, with a scrub bar. Drag to fly through five hundred pages; tap to land.
C13 · One rule at a timeA dashed rule never doubles: a block that follows a row takes the row's rule.
C14 · Tags on an arrangement tooDetails gains a Tags field; a piece's tags and an arrangement's are both filterable.
C15 · The chat box is resizableThe compose field grows with the prompt and has a grab bar to drag it taller, up to half the panel.
C16 · The score title never wrapsIn the bar the title and its subtitle are one line each and end in an ellipsis when the panel narrows the bar.

Thumbnails move to a narrow rail on the left of the page, 40 by 52pt each, and are always there in paged reading; the Pages panel is gone. The mixer is the drawer, so there is no Mixer panel either.

Foundations

Surfaces

band · the tablepanel · pages, traywell · controls, tabspaper · the score, fieldsclayclayTint

Palette unchanged. The app's ground becomes band; every screen is one or two panel pages on it with 22pt top corners, running off the bottom edge. paper is the score page and the inside of a text field. Ink at three strengths as today.

Type

LibraryPanel title 20Row name 16Key 15.5Panel item · Inter 500 14.5Meta · Inter 12.5v003 · bar 27 · mono 12

Space Grotesk for anything that names a thing; Inter for anything you read; Plex Mono for versions, bars, counts. The ladder is one step larger than 0.7 at every rung the owner called hard to read. Dynamic Type through AX3: at AX1 and above the knob group and the thumbnail rail become scrolling lists, the row's inline actions wrap to a second line inside the row.

Shape

rCtl
999 (capsule) for every button, field, chip, segment
rPage
22pt, top corners of pages and the tray
rInner
14pt for the scroll-mode page and message bubbles; 6pt for a score page; 3pt for a thumbnail
rules
1pt dashed line2 under rows and panel blocks; nothing else has a border
stamp
40pt ring, 2.5pt clay; 56/3 on a head; 30/2 in a row of a panel
shadow
only on things that float over the score: ink tools, the selection chip

Grid and centring

margins
16pt from the table's edge to a page; 24pt inside a page; 22pt inside the panel; 8pt between controls
rows
64pt; the stamp on a 40pt centre line; ordered lists add a 28pt right-aligned ordinal
centring
Inside a capsule, text and glyph centre on the capsule's geometric centre with line-height 1 and a 1pt optical lift on lowercase labels. A stamp's numeral centres 1pt left of the ring's centre because of the hash. A knob's LED is the dial's centre. A panel block that belongs to a row opens with its title on the row's centre line.
travel
A row's own actions replace its meta line inside the row. The next level opens beside, at the row's height. Nothing opens below.

Motion

panel
slides in from the right in 220ms; the page narrows in the same curve and lays out again at the new width [C8]
row actions
the meta line fades out and the actions fade in over 120ms; the row's height does not change
drawer
never animates its height; performance mode slides it off the bottom and the bar off the top together
knobs
arc follows the finger at 14pt per unit; the LED changes instantly

Accessibility

targets
44pt minimum; a 40pt capsule carries a 44pt hit area; a 32pt knob carries a 44pt group
labels
every control names its object: "Mute Violin I", "Open Versions of Sous le ciel quartet", "Back to Tuesday at the Ship"
contrast
clayStrong for small clay text; ink3 only at 12pt and above
no gate
Sign in appears in exactly two places: the Account section and a share action taken signed out

Navigation

Three depths, and the third is always the right panel. panel marks what opens beside rather than pushing.

  • Library (Pieces · Set lists · Books) — tool row: Search, Import, New, New set list · Sort panel, Filter panel, Edit (mode; also a long press on a row)
    • piece row ☰: Open · Arrangements panel · Details panel · Delete
    • set list row ☰: Open · Play · Share · Rename · Delete
    • book row ☰: Open · Rename · Delete
    • Settings (gear) — pushes; index left, section right
  • Piece — arrangements list · This piece panel (Details, Sources, New arrangement, Delete)
    • arrangement row ☰: Open · Duplicate · Move to piece panel · Delete · Versions, Parts, Details, Set lists panel
  • Set list — running order · This set list panel at rest (Add, Share, Play; People; Marks)
    • member row ☰: up · down · Open · Arrangement panel · Remove
    • Add panel · People panel · Marks panel
  • Book — pages · This book panel (Add arrangement from pages, arrangements taken out)
  • Score — bar: ‹ back · title (Versions and other arrangements panel) · layout · Pencil (ink tools) · Lasso · Chat panel · Perform · More panel (Chord symbols, Annotations, Transpose, Set lists, Share and export, Details, Settings)
    • drawer: transport, knobs, tempo, position
    • rail: pages

What it costs to build

  • New components: Panel (the right page: header, items, blocks, inline confirm), RowActions (the ☰ face), Tray (transport + knobs + tempo + position on one line, replacing ScoreFooter and MixerWindowPanel), ThumbnailRail (from PageScrubber's cache), SettingsSplit.
  • Screens that fold into the panel: Options, the ☰ management screens' bands (Versions, Parts, Details, Set lists, Move to piece), the set list's Manage bands, Add arrangements, People, Invite, Join, the version dropdown and switcher band, the OMR offer, Sort and Filter bands, Import folder. PanelDialogs becomes the inline confirm.
  • Tokens only: radii to 999 and 22, borders to clear, dashed rules, band as the ground, the ring stamp, the type ladder one step up, knob without pointer.
  • Kept as is: the engraving, page turning and follow logic, ink storage, the lasso and its addresses, chat and its model gateway, the system share sheet and file picker, the sync layer.
  • Risks: the tray at 56pt with AX text sizes (a scrolling knob list is the fallback, as MIXER_WINDOW §13 already requires); the panel at 380pt beside a 1194pt page leaves 804pt for a score, so paged reading with the panel open shows a smaller page, which is why Versions, Chat and More are the only things that open it while reading.
