Stance

The playful one, with a mechanism the others lack. The table is the palette's band; the list is a notebook page on it, 22pt corners, rows ruled with dashed lines, the numeral stamped in a clay ring. Controls are capsules. The next level is a tabbed section at the right: a white sheet with index tabs on its spine, one tab per sibling (Versions, Parts, Details, Set lists; or Sort, Filter; or Pages, Mixer, Chat), so you switch between siblings without going back, and the active tab slides to sit level with the row that opened it. A row's own actions open inside the row on paper capsules, so the finger stays on the line. One drawer at the bottom, a 56pt pencil tray with rounded shoulders: transport, knobs with the LED in the centre, tempo, position. Nothing else appears down there.

Performance mode folds everything and leaves one clay index tab at the right edge, a third of the way down, where a thumb rests on a stand. Tap it to come back.

Grid and centring

grid
16pt table margin; 24pt page margin; 8pt inside
centring
Inside a capsule everything centres on the geometric centre with line-height 1. The stamp's numeral centres optically 1pt left of the ring's centre because of the hash. Knob LEDs are the dial's centre; the active tab's centre lines up with the row's centre.

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: the same Panel with row anchoring, row-actions face and one-line drawer as Ledger and Kiln; plus the tab spine, which turns every group of sibling destinations (the ☰ screen's rows, the score's Pages, Mixer, Chat and Versions, Sort and Filter) into tabs of one sheet. The chat panel becomes a tab of the score's sheet rather than its own 380pt panel, which is the largest structural change of the three.
  • Tokens only: the table background to band, radii to 999 and 22, dashed rules, the ring stamp, capsule index.
  • Risk: vertical tab labels are hard at AX text sizes; the spine falls back to a horizontal tab row across the sheet's top at AX1 and above.
