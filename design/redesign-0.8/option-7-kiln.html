Stance

The bold one. Round 1 kept clay for numerals and rings. Kiln pours it: the library's head is a solid clay plate with white type, and the score's drawer is a solid clay bar with white glyphs and white-arc knobs, so the two things the owner said matter most (the identity, the transport) are the two things you cannot miss. Between them the paper is calm: rows on ground with soft 16pt corners, alternate rows barely lifted, the numeral a clay disc. The next level is a white sheet that slides in at the right with a 24pt shoulder and a soft shadow, and the block that belongs to your row carries a 5pt clay kiln mark down its left edge at the row's height. A row's own actions open inside the row, on well capsules. Nothing pops up at the bottom; the drawer is one 56pt line and stays one line.

Performance mode leaves a 6pt clay edge along the top, the plate's remnant. Tap it to come back.

Components sheet

Surfaces, recast

clay · plate, drawerpaper · sheet, pagegroundpanel · controlswell

Same hex values. Clay becomes a surface for the two bands that carry identity and transport; paper is used for the sheet as well as the page, because the sheet is a sheet. White on clay is 3.9:1, so drawer labels are 600 weight and glyphs, never small regular text.

Grid and centring

Labels centre by cap height with a 1pt lift; a disc's numeral centres optically 1pt left because of the hash. The kiln mark spans the block's height minus 6pt top and bottom. Knob LEDs are the dial centre.

What it costs to build

  • Changes shape: the same Panel, row-actions face and one-line drawer as Ledger; plus the plate, which replaces the library's title row, segmented control and gear with one band, and the clay drawer skin, which is the same drawer with an inverted knob palette.
  • Tokens only: radii to 12 and 16, borders to clear, the disc numeral, alternate-row lift, the clay-on-clay knob variables.
  • Risk: the drawer's white-on-clay glyphs and 9.5pt knob labels are at the contrast floor; the labels are bold and the values are for confirmation, not for reading first, and VoiceOver carries the level. The plate takes 72pt from the library; the list is still full width beneath it.
